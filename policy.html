Goal Getters FC – Password Policy

Password Complexity Rules

  • Minimum 8 characters
  • Must include at least one uppercase letter
  • Must include one number
  • Must include one special character (!@#$%^&*)
  • No use of your real name or username

Privacy Notice

Your password is encrypted and cannot be viewed or retrieved by anyone, including staff. We value your privacy and do not store passwords in readable formats. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials secure.

Note: Children under 18 must have parental or guardian consent before creating an account.

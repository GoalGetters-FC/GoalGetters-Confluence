Goal Getters FC – Frequently Asked Questions

FAQs

Do players under 18 need parental consent?

Yes, players under 18 must have a parent or legal guardian’s consent to register.

Can I try a session before enrolling?

Yes, we offer a free trial session. Contact our support team to book your slot.

What equipment do I need?

Players should bring soccer boots, shin guards, a water bottle, and training clothes. Academy gear will be provided post-registration.

How do I reset my password?

Click on \"Forgot Password\" on the login page and follow the email instructions.
